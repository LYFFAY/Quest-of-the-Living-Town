# Quest of the Living Town: A Gamified Travel Experience for Heritage Exploration

**中文名称：鲜活古城之旅：面向文化遗产探索的游戏化旅行体验**

## Project Overview | 项目概述

This project explores how gamified travel can transform heritage tourism from passive sightseeing into active cultural exploration. The student will develop a mobile prototype for a historical town, museum route, or study-tour setting, using mechanics such as quests, character roles, collectible memories, location-based puzzles, or branching storylines. The design should connect game actions with cultural interpretation rather than adding superficial rewards. Evaluation will examine how the gamified experience affects exploration behaviour, motivation, social interaction, emotional resonance, and cultural understanding. The project is suitable for tourism and museum contexts seeking engaging educational products for younger visitors and study-tour groups.

本项目探索如何通过游戏化旅行，将文化遗产旅游从被动观光转变为主动的文化探索。项目计划开发一个适用于历史城镇、博物馆路线或研学旅行场景的移动端原型，结合任务、角色扮演、记忆收集、基于位置的谜题和分支剧情等机制。游戏行为将直接服务于文化解读，而不是仅仅增加与文化内容无关的表面奖励。项目将评估这种体验对游客探索行为、参与动机、社会互动、情感共鸣和文化理解的影响，主要面向年轻游客和研学旅行团体。

## Target Users | 目标用户

- **Young visitors | 年轻游客：** 希望旅行过程有趣、有参与感，同时愿意获得文化体验。
- **Study-tour groups | 研学旅行团体：** 带着学习目标参观，需要在有限时间内理解并记住文化知识。

## Design Context | 设计场景

The initial design will focus on heritage attractions within the ancient city of Xi'an. Representative historical buildings, cultural relics, and nearby cultural locations will be connected through a story-driven route, allowing visitors to explore Xi'an's history through role-playing, puzzles, on-site tasks, and cultural interpretation.

项目初步计划以西安古城中的文化遗产景点展开设计。系统将选取具有代表性的历史建筑、文物及周边文化地点，通过剧情路线将它们连接起来，让游客在角色扮演、解谜和实地任务中探索西安的历史文化。

## Expected System | 计划开发的系统

The project will produce a mobile heritage exploration prototype. Each visit follows a story-based route through real heritage sites. Visitors interact with personified artifacts or buildings, take on roles, solve historical puzzles, complete on-site tasks, unlock cultural interpretation, and collect digital memories and 3D models.

项目计划开发一个移动端文化遗产探索原型。用户沿着由剧情组织的真实景点路线进行参观，与拟人化的文物或建筑互动，以故事角色的身份完成任务和历史谜题，并在实地探索后解锁文化解读、旅行记忆和虚拟3D模型。

## Main Features | 主要功能

### 1. Historical facts and fictional storylines | 史实与虚拟剧情结合

Each heritage site has an independent fictional storyline grounded in real historical events, people, artifacts, and architecture.

针对不同景点设计独立剧情。剧情虽然包含虚构的游戏情节，但以真实历史事件、人物、文物和建筑为基础。

### 2. Personified artifacts and architecture | 文物和建筑拟人化

Artifacts and buildings become story characters that introduce their own history through dialogue, helping reduce the psychological distance between visitors and cultural heritage.

将景点中的文物或建筑设计成故事角色，让它们通过对话介绍自己的历史和故事，拉近游客与文化遗产之间的心理距离。

### 3. Visitor role-playing | 游客参与角色扮演

Visitors become characters in the story. They interact with personified heritage characters, accept missions, and make choices that move the story forward.

用户不仅观看剧情，也会成为故事中的角色，通过与拟人化角色互动、接受任务和作出选择来推动剧情发展。

### 4. Historical puzzles connected to real routes | 解谜与实地路线结合

Historical puzzles appear throughout the story. Each answer provides clues to the next destination, connecting puzzle solving and story progression with the physical route.

剧情中穿插与历史知识相关的谜题。谜题答案会提示下一个需要前往的景点，使解谜、剧情和现实参观路线相互连接。

### 5. Adaptive puzzle difficulty | 自适应谜题难度

Visitors can choose a difficulty level based on their ability. The system aims to support different ages, knowledge levels, and gaming experience without creating frustration or boredom.

用户可以根据自己的能力选择解谜难度，使不同年龄、知识水平和游戏经验的游客都能参与，避免谜题过难产生挫败感或过于简单而失去兴趣。

### 6. Photo verification and collectible memories | 拍照验证与“收集记忆”

After reaching a target artifact or building, visitors take a photo or selfie to complete the task. Once verified, the visit becomes a collectible travel memory.

用户到达指定建筑或文物后，需要拍照或合影完成任务。系统确认照片符合要求后，将这次参观记录为一段“旅行记忆”。

### 7. Cultural interpretation unlocked through tasks | 文化解读与任务结合

Cultural information unlocks after visitors reach the real location and complete its task. Knowledge becomes an outcome of exploration instead of a block of passive instruction.

文物介绍不会一次性全部展示，而是在用户亲自到达地点并完成任务后解锁，让文化知识成为探索成果，而不是被动灌输的讲解。

### 8. 3D collections and game rewards | 3D模型收藏和游戏奖励

Completing a heritage task unlocks a corresponding virtual 3D model. Visitors can review collected models and historical information in their personal collection.

完成某个文物或建筑的任务后，用户可以获得对应的虚拟3D模型。已经解锁的模型和历史介绍可以随时在个人收藏中查看。

### 9. Main quests and optional side quests | 主线与支线任务

The main quest ends after the visitor completes all designated heritage tasks. Optional side quests may introduce smaller artifacts, distinctive nearby places, or local food without affecting main-story progress.

参观完景点内全部指定文物或建筑即完成主线。用户还可以自由选择探索周边的小型文物、特色地点或当地美食，这些支线不会影响主线进度。
